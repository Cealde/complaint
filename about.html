About Us

Our Mission

The UniHostel Complaint Portal is designed make it easy for hostellers to complain about the commodities provided in their room. Our tool directly connects students to service maintenance workers and streamline servicing rather than having to discuss the problem with the warden.

Features & Workflow

Students select their hostel, specify the details of the issue through a user-friendly categorized grid system, and track progress in real-time. Maintenance staff receive immediate updates, allowing them to allocate resources effectively and minimize downtime for critical facilities.

Administrative Office

The campus estate maintenance team supervises all operations. Warden councils from Flavus, Daphne, Hecate, Lares, Oreads, Medea, Istra, and Creon hostels work in coordination with the portal administration to audit, review, and mark complaint resolutions.
