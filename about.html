About Us

Our Mission

The UniHostel Complaint Portal is designed make it easy for hostellers to complain about the commodities provided in their room. Our tool directly connects students to service maintenance workers and streamline servicing rather than having to discuss the problem with the warden.

Features & Workflow

Students select their hostel, specify the details of the issue through a specified system, and then track the complaint's status as well as refer to past done complaints. Maintenance staff receive immediate updates, allowing them to allocate resources and maximize efficiency for fixing complaints.

Administrative Office

The Office is located near the campus. The council consists of Wardens from Flavus, Daphne, Hecate, Lares, Oreads, Medea, Istra, and Creon hostels, They all review any common issues and provide details on the hostels.

About Food -IMPORTANT
