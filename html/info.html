Information

How do I submit a complaint?

Navigate to the home page, select your hostel, and choose your complaint from the given types, then specify your complaint from the options, if your complaint is not mentioned then mention the details in the additional field.
Enter your room number and the rest of your details, once the complaint has been recieved you will get a conformation email on your registered email.

How can I track my complaint status?

Click on "My Complaints" in the top navigation bar. You will see a table listing all your submitted complaints and their current status, whether they have been Sent, Recieved or Resolved.

What do the different complaint priorites mean?

Emergency Complaint: is to be only used when there is an issue which makes the room unlivable or Life Threatening such as a Water Flood or Room Electricity Outage.
Priority Complaint: is to be used when the issue is largely inconvinient but the room is still habitable such as Fan Malfunction or a Broken Door.
Standard Complaint: is to be used for every other problem which results in a minor inconvinience such as a broken mirror or water leaks.

Misuse

Submitting false complaints or intentionally giving the wrong details about a complaint will first be met with a warning then any repeats will be met with a fine or disciplinary action such as suspension or dismissal based on the severity and repeat of the complaint.

Processing

Once the complaint has been recieved, before the worker is sent to service your room you will get a phone call on your given phone number, please answer the call to confirm the complaint as well as elaborate on the complaint if needed, provide additional details to make the servicing go smoothly such as the PIN for the room padlock(if applicable) but try to make sure that your room remains unlocked after submitting the complaint.

Subpar Servicing

If your given room has not been serviced within the timeframe or if all the given issues were not fully fixed, contact the admin team on the phone no. in the contact us page to elaborate on the issue and provide the Issue ID then we will proceed to profusely apologize then afterwards send a service worker to re-resolve the issue.
